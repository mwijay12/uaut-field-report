01 — Retail and supermarket operating system

Faida POS

Sell anywhere. Work offline. Keep complete control.

Open the live demo

Point of sale and inventory for shops, supermarkets, pharmacies, cafes and hardware stores. It keeps selling with no internet and syncs when the connection returns.

  • Offline

    Fully offline-first. Sales continue with zero internet and sync automatically once online.

  • Checkout

    Barcode checkout in under 0.8 seconds on average, with instant thermal receipts.

  • Stock

    Low-stock warnings and inventory depletion alerts.

  • Money

    M-Pesa and mobile-money reconciliation, daily sales summaries, profit margins and an audit trail.

  • Devices

    A Flutter Android app for store attendants and a web terminal for owners.

02 — Attendance and health logging for care

CareTrack

Attendance, health notes and guardian trust.

Open the live demo

For daycare centres, preschools, health clinics, community centres and NGOs. Attendance takes one tap, and guardians hear about it by SMS.

  • Attendance

    One-tap child attendance with automatic SMS notifications to guardians.

  • Health

    Daily health notes, medication logs and meal records for each child.

  • Fees

    Fee management with M-Pesa payment tracking and automatic receipts.

  • Privacy

    Encrypted storage, designed around Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Act of 2022.

  • Network

    Works on weak 2G and 3G connections.
