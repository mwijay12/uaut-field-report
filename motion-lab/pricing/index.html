Maswali — Questions

Plain answers

How much does a website cost with Altraware?

Web Starter is TSh {{site.price.starter}} and is delivered in 1 to 2 weeks. Growth Business Site is TSh {{site.price.growth}} and Pro Web Portal is TSh {{site.price.portal}}. Custom apps start from TSh {{site.price.app}}, and turnkey business systems like Faida POS from TSh {{site.price.system}}. Every project has a written agreement and clear deliverables.

Do your apps and systems work without internet?

Yes. Faida POS and our custom operational systems are built offline-first. Your staff can sell, record transactions and check stock with no internet at all. The data is stored safely on the device and syncs by itself when a connection returns.

How long does a project take?

Websites take 1 to 2 weeks. Web applications start at 4 weeks, Flutter mobile apps take 4 to 6 weeks, and full business systems take about 6 to 10 weeks.

Do you train our staff?

Yes. Every system includes hands-on training, either at your premises or by video, with illustrated PDF manuals and video walkthroughs your team can watch again at any time.

Who owns the source code and the data?

You do. When the project is complete we hand over the code repositories, credentials, databases and assets, as set out in the written agreement.
